Our Story

Band History

Carmilla was formed by our beloved frontwoman and lead guitarist, Star, as a passion project of hers to channel all of the innovative gothic song ideas she has, whether it's deathrock, gothic rock, gothabilly, or even surf rock. The rest of us loved her idea and jumped in the bandwagon, and the rest is history. We've all been working really hard to produce the most eerie and shocking music, all while balancing our studies as STEM majors in college!

Musical Influences

  • Christian Death
  • 45 Grave
  • The Cramps
  • Bauhaus

Our initial influences include Christian Death, 45 Grave, as well as several other deathrock artists, and lately we've taken inspiration from bands like The Creepniks and Bone Orchard for our recent gothic psychobilly song, Case of the Rabies!
